# Device Retirement Process

## 1. Trigger
A device may be retired if it fails, reaches its refresh cycle, is no longer needed because an employee has left, or has been lost.

## 2. Unenroll
Once the device is removed from Intune, it will no longer be counted as a managed device. A device that stops checking in should be marked as missing, not retired.

## 3. Recover
Recover the device license and any assigned software.

## 4. Destroy the Data
Use an approved data destruction method, such as:
- Cryptographic erasure on an encrypted drive
- Secure wipe to a defined standard
- Physical destruction

For a bank that stores customer nonpublic information, the chosen method should meet the organization's security and regulatory requirements.

## 5. Obtain the Certificate
Request a Certificate of Destruction from the vendor that includes:
- Device serial numbers
- Date of destruction

Without this certificate, the bank cannot prove that the disposal was completed properly.

## 6. Update the Inventory
Update the inventory records to reflect the device's actual status. Keeping inventory records accurate is critical, otherwise the inventory system will produce incorrect information.
